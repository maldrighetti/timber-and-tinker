# Before you build

These plans are for education and recreation. They are not professional engineering, architectural, or construction advice.

Follow the manufacturer’s instructions for every tool and product you use, including minimum age, training, protective equipment, and any supervision the manufacturer requires. Do not use a tool in a way its maker does not allow.

Unless a project expressly says it is designed to bear a person’s weight, do not walk, stand, sit, or climb on it.

Woodworking involves sharp tools, flying debris, and other risks of injury. You are responsible for choosing a suitable project, using tools safely, inspecting materials, and deciding whether a build is right for your skill and setting. If you are not comfortable with a step or a tool, stop and get help from someone experienced before you continue.
